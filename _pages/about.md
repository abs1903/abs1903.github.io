I am an undergraduate student in Intelligent Science and Technology at Peking University and a member of the Zhi Class (Experimental Program in Intelligent Science and Technology), 2024 cohort.

My current research interests focus on autonomous driving, AI agents, embodied AI, and multimodal learning. I co-authored the CVPR 2026 paper KnowVal: A Knowledge-Augmented and Value-Guided Autonomous Driving System as second author, and I have also worked on DrivingAgent and HiDrive.

I enjoy building systems that connect ideas, models, and evaluation into complete research workflows. My recent work includes knowledge-augmented autonomous driving, agent framework design, and identity-preserving image generation.

Selected highlights
- CVPR 2026 second-author paper: KnowVal
- Research on DrivingAgent and HiDrive
- Project lead of a PKU SEECS Innovation+ workstation project
- CCPC National Finals Gold Medal (Harbin 2024) and Silver Medal (Chongqing 2025)
- Tianchuang Scholarship, PKU SEECS (2024–2025)
